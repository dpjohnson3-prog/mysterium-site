Mysterium

A traditional Catholic rosary: the daily mysteries, in Latin and English, with sacred paintings and Procter's meditations.

Frequently asked questions

How do haptics work?
Each bead gives a small tap as you advance, with a stronger pulse at the end of a decade and a gentle confirmation when the rosary is complete. You can turn haptics off in Settings. Haptics need an iPhone; they are not available on iPad.
How do reminders work?
Reminders are optional. Choose a time in Settings and Mysterium schedules a notification on your device. Novena reminders work the same way. Nothing is sent from a server, and you can turn reminders off at any time in Settings or in iOS Settings → Notifications.
Can I pray in Latin?
Yes. In Settings choose English, Latin, or both side by side.
How do streaks work?
Finishing a rosary counts that day. Consecutive days build your streak, and the calendar shows each day you prayed. Streaks are stored only on your device, so deleting the app erases them.
Which mysteries are prayed each day?
Monday and Thursday Joyful, Tuesday and Friday Sorrowful, Wednesday and Saturday Glorious. On Sundays you can follow the liturgical season or always pray the Glorious Mysteries.
